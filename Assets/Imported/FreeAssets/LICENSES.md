# Bundled free environment assets

## ambientCG — Ground 037

- Source: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground037
- Downloaded format: 2K JPG PBR maps
- License: CC0 1.0; attribution is not required.
- Used in game: color and DirectX normal maps texture the clearing floor. Roughness and ambient-occlusion maps are included for future material tuning.

## Poly Haven — Rock 09

- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_09
- Downloaded format: 2K FBX plus 2K diffuse, DirectX normal, roughness, and OpenGL normal maps
- License: CC0; attribution is not required. Poly Haven is credited here as the source.
- Used in game: four detailed trail boulders share the imported mesh and a shared URP material. Distant boulders remain simple meshes to keep the prototype's geometry cost modest.

Both asset files are bundled locally and have no runtime network dependency. No paid content was downloaded.
